1.2 Career Aspirations and Professional Development

1.2.1 My Career Aspirations

I am currently pursuing a Bachelor’s degree in Life Sciences, specializing in Data Science for Biology. My interest in biology and medical developments, combined with my scientific background, has motivated me to explore career opportunities that extend beyond traditional laboratory research.

While I value my practical laboratory experience, I envision a career in which I can combine my scientific knowledge with communication, business, and advisory responsibilities. I am particularly interested in positions such as Solutions Consultant, Technical Sales Specialist, or Business Development Specialist within the Life Sciences industry.

My ideal job would involve interacting with clients, understanding their challenges, presenting technical or scientific solutions, and building professional relationships. I would also enjoy attending conferences, visiting companies, and travelling internationally for business purposes. Ideally, I would like to work in a hybrid environment that offers flexibility, opportunities for professional growth, and attractive salary prospects.

Over the next one to two years, I hope to gain further experience and explore opportunities that will help me transition towards this type of career.

1.2.2 My Current Skills and Experience

Throughout my studies and professional experience, I have developed a foundation in laboratory techniques, biological sciences, and data analysis.

Having worked in three different laboratories, I have gained practical experience in scientific environments and an understanding of laboratory workflows, experimental procedures, and the importance of reliable data.

Through my Data Science specialization, I have developed skills in R and RStudio, biological data analysis, and bioinformatics. I have also gained experience with Git, GitHub, and basic Linux and Bash commands.

In addition to my technical background, I consider communication, adaptability, and interpersonal skills to be personal strengths. I enjoy interacting with people and working in environments where I can exchange ideas and collaborate with others.

1.2.3 Skills I Want to Develop

To move towards my career goals, I would like to further develop both my technical and professional skills.

One area I want to improve is the ability to communicate complex scientific information in a clear, accessible, and visually engaging way. This is particularly relevant for advisory and client-facing positions, where technical information often needs to be explained to people with different levels of scientific knowledge.

For this reason, I have chosen to focus on interactive dashboard development using R Shiny during the independent learning component of the Workflows course. My goal is to learn how to transform biological datasets into interactive visualizations that allow users to explore and interpret scientific information.

Additionally, I would like to improve my presentation, negotiation, and business communication skills. I believe that combining scientific expertise, data literacy, and strong interpersonal skills will help me prepare for a career that connects science, technology, and business.
